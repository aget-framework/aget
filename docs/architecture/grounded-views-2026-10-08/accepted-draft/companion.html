One architecture, two grounded views.

The overview separates the Principal, persistent Aget identity, working copy, records, runtime and collaboration session. The technology view adds operation permission control and execution evidence. Both use the same adopted meanings.

Meaning and evidence have different jobs.

These architectural meanings were adopted for this model after independent review and scoped checks. The diagrams remain publication drafts. An arrow defines a conditional relationship; it does not show that a particular installation, permission decision or execution occurred.

The pinned public framework sources are supporting context, not a claim that this extended model is already a published framework standard. This is SKOS-aligned vocabulary with explicit scoped constraints, not a complete logical axiomatization.

Nine architectural concepts

Diagram labels are descriptive reading labels. The registered labels, full definitions and genera below anchor their meaning. Spaces replace underscores and field-edge whitespace is trimmed for reading; the JSON preserves complete adopted definition fields, including their original whitespace.

Principal

Registered meaning: Principal

A Principal is a Cognitive Entity that AI agents serve and that retains ultimate authority over their system.

Genus: Cognitive Entity. A Cognitive Entity is an Entity capable of performing cognitive processes, such as perception, memory, reasoning, learning, or decision-making.

Evidence limit: No actual approval event or blanket requirement for a new approval at every action is established.

The pinned public vocabulary describes a human operator. That supports the human collaboration case; the adopted model uses the broader cognitive-entity genus without claiming that every Principal is human.

Aget identity

Registered meaning: Aget Instance

An Aget Instance is an Agent created from a reusable template and configured as a persistent identity distinct from its working copies and the AI system it uses.

Genus: Agent. An entity that perceives some part of its environment and acts on it in pursuit of goals — its own, or those of another party on whose behalf it acts. The genus is neutral about substrate: people, organizations, software and collectives can all be agents. What sets an agent apart from other entities is directed action, not what it is made of.

Evidence limit: No live identity reconciliation, uninterrupted memory or successful handoff is proved.

Collaboration session

Registered meaning: Collaboration Session

A Collaboration Session is a Channel Interaction that forms a bounded principal-agent or pairing work episode.

Genus: Channel Interaction. An event that is a bounded communicative exchange among participants over a communication channel — e.g., a meeting, thread, pairing session, DM exchange, or review. The perdurant unit at which collaboration episodes are scored.

Evidence limit: The definition does not show that a particular episode completed or recovered context correctly.

Full embedded parent meanings

Agent: An entity that perceives some part of its environment and acts on it in pursuit of goals — its own, or those of another party on whose behalf it acts. The genus is neutral about substrate: people, organizations, software and collectives can all be agents. What sets an agent apart from other entities is directed action, not what it is made of.

Artifact: An entity intentionally produced by an agent to serve a purpose, whose identity depends on its author's productive intention and intended function rather than on natural-kind membership. Artifacts are evaluated against the purposes they were designed to fulfill.

Channel Interaction: An event that is a bounded communicative exchange among participants over a communication channel — e.g., a meeting, thread, pairing session, DM exchange, or review. The perdurant unit at which collaboration episodes are scored.

Cognitive Entity: A Cognitive Entity is an Entity capable of performing cognitive processes, such as perception, memory, reasoning, learning, or decision-making.

Entity: An Entity is a Thing whose existence the describing ontology assumes, whether concrete or abstract and individual or collective, and that can bear properties and stand in relations to other entities.

Event: Something that happens at a specific time, with a beginning and an end; a bounded occurrent that may involve persons, be hosted by an organization, and produce documents or result in decisions. (L459 core entity, here promoted from vision YAML to instantiated concept.)

Occurrent: An entity that unfolds through time, having temporal parts and a beginning and end — as opposed to a continuant, which is wholly present at every moment of its existence. The top category under which events, processes, sessions, and activities fall.

Software System: A system for which software is of primary importance to the stakeholders — a combination of interacting elements (software, hardware, data, human procedures) organized to achieve one or more stated purposes, where software is the principal locus of functionality. The unit of process governance under ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207:2017.

System: A combination of interacting elements organized to achieve one or more stated purposes, where the whole exhibits properties not reducible to its components considered in isolation. A system is bounded, structured, and goal-directed.

Thing: A Thing is anything that can be referred to, named, or made the subject of discourse, whether concrete or abstract.

A concurrency example

Two sessions can use the same working copy. A recently written evidence record for one session cannot certify the other.
