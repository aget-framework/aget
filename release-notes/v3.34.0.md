## Known limitations and regression coverage

The accepted successor payload is commit `1a87e0743c42727aab36128911fc8a4c89692398`, tree `0a0058a4366dd5428ac6b8e2b2a69c49d355bed5`. Its sole parent, original seal `72633e6d1b58e94541a59892d30c0b4bbd9d20f1`, remains immutable. One successor incorporates the independently reviewed 21-path repair delta. Release packaging adds metadata and handoff artifacts.

Original acceptance recorded a regression-coverage shortfall: RM-3, RM-4, RM-8 and RM-11 survived the original shipped source-substring tests, while independent non-shipping cases rejected all four. That review exercised 27 discriminating checks plus one observation. Successor repairs replace two brittle source assertions with behavioral checks and add rejecting cases for discovered defects. The four historical mutation classes have not all been re-scored against the successor; their disclosure and next-cycle verification obligation remain carried, rather than being declared closed by the new test count.

The lint ratchet passes at 436 findings against the unchanged 437 baseline with Ruff 0.15.12; inherited debt remains. Formatting-only transformations were proven AST-identical before separately reviewed import and behavioral changes. The source-bound successor suite passed 973 tests with 11 disclosed skips and 13 passing subtests. All nine new instruments were run: missing host/runtime/receiver evidence, the raw-template parsing limit and static-audit classification limits remain explicit. A missing tool, subject or required input is not a pass.

Original independent review used a separate actor with producer-hosted permission handling; its permission, coaching and custody limitations remain recorded. Renewed full-delta acceptance used a separate reviewer context on the same harness/model family and machine. That reviewer made no candidate edits and independently verified the successor commit/tree binding. Candidate acceptance is distinct from final release-quality acceptance, publication and receiver delivery.

## Upgrade and compatibility

Read [the release handoff](../handoffs/RELEASE_HANDOFF_v3.34.0.md) and [deployment specification](../DEPLOYMENT_SPEC_v3.34.0.yaml) from the peeled public tag before installation.
